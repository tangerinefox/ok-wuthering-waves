Review
Hsin is a 5-star Electro Rectifier Main DPS who plays into the Electro Flare archetype and a (sort of) brand new one, Unison. Electro Flare is one of the many Negative Statuses, and all of them have received significant attention meta-wise recently. However, Unison is a previously forgotten mechanic only Jinhsi possessed up until now, that involves your character doing 2 Outros per rotation: Unison gives one for free, and your Concerto gauge gives you the other. In the same vein as how Jinhsi plays, this creates more unique team rotations compared to the usual. With that being said, Hsin’s kit has many complex elements based on her Resonance Mode, so we’ll have to go in-depth to fully understand it.
Key Mechanics & Rotation Concepts
Hsin has 3 states that she will switch between during battle throughout her rotation: Answering Form, Illumining Form, and Mechanism Dominion. In Answering Form, you build up the Forte required to switch to Illumining Form, then in that form, you build up the Forte that is required to enter Mechanism Dominion, at which point you consume said Forte to then unleash Hsin’s final Ultimate. Pretty straightforward idea.
However, we can go a bit more in-depth on the details here, based on Hsin’s resonance mode. They fundamentally change how she will rotate and what kinds of synergies she will get.
In Electro Flare mode, Hsin will perform a standard 1-2-3 team rotation like in any classic team you’ve previously played, so she’ll do her on-field rotation fully until her Outro is ready and all her key abilities have been cast. Flare mechanics work in this way:
Electro Flare stacks normally get consumed every time the instance of periodic damage it should deal is triggered. When Hsin is on the team, this is not the case. Instead, Hsin will have any extra Electro Flare stacks over the stack limit (10 at base) converted into Heart of Thunder instead of Electro Rage, to then unleash a detonation of its damage in one Skill.
Electro Flare is applied by: Her first Ultimate, Forte Skill, her Basics after her Forte Skill, and her second Forte Heavy (5 stacks each), as well as her Intro (1 stack).
Once Hsin casts her first Ultimate, she will apply 1 stack of Electro Flare to enemies who have none. If another teammate applies at least 10 Flare, Hsin stores this information and raises the Flare count up to the max instead. These effects apply to all nearby enemies until her rotation ends.
Hsin gains extra Electro DMG Bonus when another character in the team applies Electro Flare onto enemies, with an extra boost if that character is Electro Rover.
In Unison mode, Hsin rotates quite differently. Unison provides her with a free Outro once every 25s when her first Ultimate is cast, at the cost of no Concerto energy. Other mechanics include:
Hsin increases the DMG of all Unison Response characters, including herself, by stacking up a unique buff (somewhat similar to Tune Strain) called Unison Boon. This buff is given when Unison Response characters perform Intros with each other;
Hsin can entirely skip the Illumining Form state and go straight into Mechanism Dominion if she Intros again after her First Ultimate;
Hsin’s Intros are enhanced if they are triggered by another Unison character and they give Hsin 50% more ATK for 8s.
In other words, you are heavily incentivized to use Unison mode with and only with other Unison characters. To add to this, Hsin’s Outro gives 20% DMG amplification to other characters in the team than herself, but only to Electro DMG (Electro Flare mode) or Unison characters (Unison mode).
Hence, we can conclude Hsin’s rotation will have her stacking up her Forte gauges (which she can only do while she is active on the field, so Swap Cancelling is restricted) and entering Illumining Form via her Ultimate. In Unison mode, she will switch out until another Unison character can give her another Intro. In Flare mode, she will stay on field and charge up her second Forte, both things done to enter Mechanism Dominion state, at which point she can do a few Basic Attacks to consume the Forte, cast her second Forte Heavy and Ultimate, and then Outro until her next rotation starts.
Meta Position & Conclusion
You may have guessed the following when we described the Unison state mechanics:
Electro Flare is Hsin’s more flexible mode teammates-wise, and her most F2P-friendly.
Currently, the only Unison character available is Jinhsi, and she cannot fully activate all the buffs in Hsin’s kit. Not to mention, the team is rather weak compared to the rest of the current meta. In other words, new teammates are to come for this Resonance Mode.
And this is entirely true. Performance-wise, Hsin’s Flare teams are however still restricted to using Electro Rover and Suisui at best outside of Quickswap (where Chisa would be privileged instead). Overall, Electro Rover is far from an ideal character, with low total buffing potential and a lengthy rotation, meaning they too could be replaced in the future. But for now, they are enough to make Hsin a good DPS still, and a budget-friendly one at that, able to run Buling in the 3rd slot for a nearly cost-free team composition while still retaining good enough performance to clear endgame modes when played properly.
For this reason though, raw performance-wise, Hsin is below the rest of the modern 3.x cast. Luuk can expect to see a bit higher results than her too. With that being said, Hsin has a way out in the form of Unison Mode, which has not been expanded upon yet. We can expect Kuro to release more Unison characters to synergize with Hsin to bring her up to modern performance standards. With that being said, we have no guarantee of this, so right now Hsin is simply a more budget-friendly Flare DPS than the rest of the modern cast, being able to take advantage of Rover better than any other character currently, who is accessible to everyone and doesn’t need their Signature weapon.
In conclusion, while Hsin has no good permanent banner weapons, with her and her Signature, you essentially have a guaranteed Flare team for her out of the box, and that makes her a great pull for beginner players. In the future, she may be subject to receiving additional firepower through Unison, but for now her performance ceiling is quite a bit below her peers. Keep this into account when choosing whether to pull for her or not.

Additional Tips & Tricks
Hsin is a very straightforward character with very little potential for Swap Cancels. The most she could get done is swapping out of her Skill in Answering Form (her base form) before her rotation starts, to swap back and get off a Basic 4 pre-Intro while some other character is busy doing something else. However, she needs to be on-field in order for any Forte to be generated. This makes her very restricted to a nearly completely strict on-field rotation, and overall it’s very difficult to utilize any form of Quickswap with Hsin herself (this will mostly happen through her teammates).
The Skills that are swapped at the end of each rotation before Outro are simply there for free extra damage (this goes for the first half of the Unison rotation too), and can be skipped if you’re uncomfortable with fast Swap Cancels. Do them too slowly, and they stop being DPS increases.
Synergies
Rover (Electro) Jinhsi - Electro Rover is Hsin's current best second slot teammate, as they provide her with some external Electro Flare application which is great for triggering her passives, as well as being able to provide her with a massive amount of Electro DMG Bonus among some other buffs. Jinhsi is the only current Unison character in the game, so she's the only option for Unison mode Hsin to be used with normally, but generally she's not recommended even over a F2P Flare team.
The Shorekeeper Suisui Chisa Buling - Shorekeeper is Unison Hsin's best current Support as there is no dedicated Support for Unison teams, so she comes out on top by virtue of being the best generalist choice. Suisui, Chisa and Buling are all options for Electro Flare Hsin. Suisui is generally the strongest, but Chisa can outperform her in Quickswap. Buling is the F2P fallback option, giving extra Flare application and Skill DMG-oriented buffs.
